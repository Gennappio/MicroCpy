# 10.2 Limitations

OpenCellComms does not prove that a biological model is correct.

It can validate structure, not truth. A workflow can be well organized and still use
the wrong rate, the wrong threshold, or the wrong biological assumption.

The system also depends on the quality of the authoring protocol. If the model
intake is incomplete, the coding agent may still make poor choices. The point of the
protocol is to expose those choices, not to remove the need for domain expertise.

Finally, node-based organization can introduce overhead and may not be ideal for all
high-performance simulation settings. The current goal is understandable,
modifiable, research-oriented modeling, not maximum HPC performance.
