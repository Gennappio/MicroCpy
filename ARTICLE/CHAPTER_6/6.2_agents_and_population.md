# 6.2 Agents and Population

An Agent represents one biological individual, such as one cell. It stores
per-agent state and provides methods for reading and updating that state.

The Population is the collective that owns the agents. It is responsible for
creating, wrapping, removing, and reconciling agents. Creation functions work at the
population level. Step behaviors work at the single-agent level.

This separation prevents a common modeling error. A per-agent behavior should not
loop over all cells. It should act on the one bound agent. Collective operations,
such as creating a population from a file, belong in creation.
