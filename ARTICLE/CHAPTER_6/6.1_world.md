# 6.1 World

The World represents spatial structure. It knows the geometry, topology,
neighborhoods, occupancy, and spatial queries.

For a biologist, the World is the place where the model happens. It can represent a
lattice, bounded or periodic topology, and neighborhood relations. Agent and
resource functions can ask the World for spatial information rather than
reimplementing spatial logic in every node.

This keeps node-functions small. A migration function should describe the biological
rule for movement, not rebuild the geometry of the simulation.
