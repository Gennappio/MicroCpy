# 6.3 Resources and Fields

Resources represent fields on the world. They may be discrete fields, such as sugar
in a Sugarscape model, or continuous diffusing substances, such as oxygen,
chemokines, glucose, or TNF.

By treating substances as first-class resources, OpenCellComms gives biologists one
place to inspect them. Their initialization and behavior are homed under Resources.
This makes questions such as "where is this signal produced and consumed?" easier
to answer.

Some field operations are local to one resource. Others are coupled, such as a
multi-substance diffusion solve. The workflow can represent both, while keeping the
ownership visible.
