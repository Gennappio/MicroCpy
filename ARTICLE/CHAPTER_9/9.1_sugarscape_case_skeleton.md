# 9.1 Sugarscape Case Skeleton

Sugarscape is the simplest case study.

It demonstrates:

- a world or lattice;
- forager agents;
- sugar as a resource;
- resource regrowth;
- per-agent movement and consumption;
- scheduler order;
- simple plots or summaries.

The chapter should show how a classic ABM maps cleanly into OpenCellComms:

```text
World
  setup lattice

Resource
  sugar
    initialize sugar
    grow back sugar

Agent
  forager
    create foragers
    move
    eat sugar
    metabolize

Scheduler
  ask foragers
  grow sugar
  reconcile
  report
```

Main message: OpenCellComms can express a classic ABM as small visible nodes rather
than as a monolithic script.
