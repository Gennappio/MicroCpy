# 9. Use Cases Overview

This chapter should present three use cases that illustrate increasing model
complexity:

- Sugarscape: a minimal ABM with agents and a discrete resource;
- TCELL_CORRAL: immune-cell behavior, spatial interaction, and signaling;
- MicroC: multi-scale coupling of cell behavior, fields, and intracellular state.

The goal is not only to show that the models run. The goal is to show that the same
OpenCellComms organization can make different ABM families readable, editable, and
validatable by biologists.
